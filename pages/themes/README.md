# Themes

Themes demonstrate practical research applications of Globalise.

## Possible themes

- Animal studies
- Cultural history
- Commodity history
- Diplomatic history
- Environmental history
- Food history
- Genealogical history
- Gender history
- Slavery history
- Social history
- History of science
- Medicine and health
- Technology
- Visual and material culture

This list is non-exhaustive.

## Contributor template

Individual Theme pages should normally explain:

1. Why this theme is worth researching.
2. Which research questions Globalise can help investigate.
3. Which data, entities and search functions are useful.
4. How to formulate searches.
5. Example documents and archival material.
6. Limitations and biases.
7. Further reading and resources.

Substantive Theme pages will be commissioned from external contributors.
