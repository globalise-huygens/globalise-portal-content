# Explore

Not sure where to start? Explore offers two routes into the Globalise material. You can browse the archive and the people, places, ships and concepts recorded in it, or follow a research guide that shows how others have used Globalise to investigate a historical topic.

Looking for something specific? [Search all documents](/search).

## Explore the data

<!-- Implementation note: each tile below is rendered as a tile and links directly to the matching results tab in Search (the archive tile links to the Catalogue). URLs to be added by the development team. -->

**The archive**
Browse the VOC archives by inventory, series and document.

**Places**
Towns, forts, rivers, islands and other places mentioned in the archives, with their historical and present-day names.

**Persons**
People who appear in the archives, from Company servants to rulers, free burghers and enslaved people.

**Ships**
Company ships that sailed between the Netherlands and Asia.

**Polities & rulers**
States and other polities that existed between 1600 and 1815, and their rulers.

**Organisations**
Company settlements, administrative areas and governing bodies, and other organisations that appear in the archives.

**Thesaurus**
Concepts used to describe the archives, such as commodities, occupations, ethnicities, religious groups, weights, measures and currencies.

How the reference data was compiled, and what it does and does not cover, is described in [Reference data & entity linking](/pages/research-responsibly/data/reference-data.md).

## Research guides

Research guides show how Globalise can be used to investigate a historical question, a group of people, a place or a type of source. They are written by researchers, often from outside the Globalise team, and each guide names its author.
