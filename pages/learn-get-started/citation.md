Content to be developed.

## Findings made with an AI assistant

If you found material through an AI assistant connected to the [Globalise MCP server](/pages/learn-get-started/ai-assistant.md), cite the documents and scans themselves, by their identifiers, as you would cite anything else you found on the portal. Do not cite the assistant or the conversation as a source. Also note the version of the search index that the server reports (for example `annotated-documents.2026.11.05`), because search results can change when the index is updated. If an AI assistant played a substantial part in your research, say so, and describe how you used it.
