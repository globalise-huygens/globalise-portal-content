# Archive

This section preserves documents and blog posts that were published while the Globalise project was under way, on the former [project website](https://globalise.huygens.knaw.nl/) and [documentation website](https://docs.globalise.huygens.knaw.nl/). Together they show how the project developed: from the first ideas about datasets and text recognition in 2022, through data sprints, user panels and experiments, to reflections on working with colonial archives as the project approached its end.

## About these pages

The texts are reproduced as they were originally published. They have not been edited or updated, so they reflect the state of the project, and the views of their authors, at the time of writing. Some of the information in them has since been superseded. For example, posts refer to the "GLOBALISE" spelling of the project's name, to a temporary transcriptions viewer, and to plans and dates that may since have changed. For current information about the project, see the other pages in the [About Globalise](/pages/about/index.md) section.

Each archived page states where and when it was first published. Short notes about the authors have been added at the top of each blog post, based on information from the former project website. Links to other blog posts point to their archived copies on this portal. Other links point to their original destinations and may no longer work.
