# About Globalise

This research portal is the main outcome of the Globalise project, which made one of the most important archival series of the Dutch East India Company (VOC) searchable and researchable. The series contains almost 5 million handwritten pages written between the early seventeenth and the late eighteenth century. They document not only the VOC itself but also the societies of Asia, Africa and the wider Indian Ocean world with which the company traded, negotiated and fought, and which it colonised.

The pages in this section explain what the Globalise project was, what it set out to do and what it achieved, where it stands in relation to the VOC and colonial history, how the material on this portal was produced and who made it. They are written for anyone who wants to understand where the portal's content comes from before using it in research, teaching or other work.

## Before you start using the portal

The Globalise project worked with colonial archives. The documents were written by and for the VOC, and they reflect its interests, assumptions and categories. Much of the information on this portal has also been produced or enriched by computational methods, which make mistakes and can reproduce the biases of the sources. We recommend reading the pages on [Ethics & colonial context](/pages/research-responsibly/ethics/index.md) alongside this section.

If you are new to the VOC or its archives, the [guide to the VOC archives](/pages/research-responsibly/voc-archives-guide.md) gives a practical introduction to the company, its record-keeping and what the Globalise project made available. For technical details about the data and how to access it, see [Data & documentation](/pages/research-responsibly/data/index.md).
